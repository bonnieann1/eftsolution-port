My story

You’re intelligent, capable, and still allowed to need support.

In 2006, I was running a successful business with three young children, yet found myself caught in patterns I couldn’t think my way out of. Despite knowing all the “right” strategies — the law of attraction, productivity systems, visualization — nothing was working the way it was supposed to.

The overwhelm and anxiety kept building. My relationships suffered. My health declined. I was coping, but only just.

When I finally worked with someone who understood energy healing, everything changed — not in months, but in weeks. I discovered that lasting change requires more than strategy or willpower. It requires addressing the emotional patterns and energetic blocks running beneath our conscious awareness.

That experience set me on a 20-year journey into energy healing. I’ve trained across EFT, NLP, Quantum Touch, channeled healing, remote healing, energy psychology, life coaching, and modern stress management.

Credentials matter less than results. I work intuitively, drawing on whichever approach serves you best in the moment. Sometimes that’s tapping. Sometimes it’s channeled energy work. Sometimes it’s remote healing while you sleep. I follow what your energy needs.

How I work

Practical and profound, in the same room.

All sessions are conducted remotely over Zoom. I hold space for the practical and the profound — for the unique pressures facing entrepreneurs and high-achievers, and for the deeper wisdom of energy work.
